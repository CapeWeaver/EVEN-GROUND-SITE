# Partner page — content request

*Send this to a partner organisation alongside a link to their draft page. One
template for all five partners, so every page ends up cohesive.*

**Total ask: about 450–600 words and 7–9 photographs.** Most of it already exists
in the partner's own annual report, website, or funder updates — this is a
gathering exercise, not a writing one.

---

## How to use this

Every partner page on evenground.org is built from the same eight slots, in the
same order. That structure is already live for all five partners, drafted from
each organisation's own public material. **So the ask is "confirm or correct",
not "write from scratch".**

Send the partner:

1. The link to their draft page.
2. This form, with the *existing* draft text already pasted into each field.

They then either tick it off or replace it. That is a ten-minute job for them
instead of an afternoon, and it keeps every page's shape identical.

**Two slots are Even Ground's to write, not the partner's** — §7 (Even Ground's
role) and the closing "Integrated" card in §5. Don't ask partners for those.

Word counts are the real ranges measured across the five live pages. Treat them
as targets, not limits — but a field at three times the count will not fit the
design and will come back for trimming.

## §1 · Header

| Field | What it is | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **Organisation name** | Exactly as it should appear in print | — |
| **Tagline** | One line: what the organisation is for. Not a slogan — a plain description | **10–20 words** |
| **Location** | Community and province, as a visitor would place it | e.g. *KwaNzimakwe, KwaZulu-Natal* |
| **Founded** | Year | 4 digits |
| **Partner since** | Year Even Ground's support began | 4 digits |
| **Focus** | 3–4 keywords, separated by `·` | e.g. *ECD · Nutrition · Literacy · Farming* |

> Live example — Thanda: *"Holistic, rural childhood development in KwaZulu-Natal:
> from early learning to youth leadership."*

---

## §2 · Four numbers

Four headline figures, each with a short label. These animate as counters, so
each one must be **a single number**, not a range or a sentence.

| # | Number | Label (3–6 words) | Year / period | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | |
| 2 | | | | |
| 3 | | | | |
| 4 | | | | |

Rules that come from the design:
- A plain integer, optionally with `+` (`200,000+`) or `%`. No "approx.", no "1–2".
- Say what period it covers — "meals served **yearly**", "books borrowed **in 2024**".
- One of the four is usually the relationship itself: *"16 — Years of Even Ground partnership"*. Even Ground can fill that one in.
- Three is acceptable if the fourth would be padding. Never more than four.

---

## §3 · Opening statement

**A headline** (4–8 words) — the single idea the page argues.
Live examples: *"Inclusion begins before school does"* · *"Smart with heart"* ·
*"Equality of opportunity begins with the classroom"*.

**A lead paragraph** — who you serve, where, and the shape of your approach.
**20–40 words.**

---

## §4 · The fuller story

**Two paragraphs, 25–60 words each.**

- **Paragraph 1 — how you got here.** Founding intent, and what changed as the work matured.
- **Paragraph 2 — what you run now.** The programmes as a single sentence-length list, so the reader knows the scope before §5 breaks it down.

---

## §5 · Programmes

**Two to five programmes.** Each one:

| Field | Length |
|---|---|
| Programme name | 2–5 words |
| Who it serves | ages or group, e.g. *Birth → 4 years*, *Girls and young women* |
| What happens in it | **15–35 words** |

```
Programme 1
  Name:
  Serves:
  Description:

Programme 2
  Name:
  Serves:
  Description:
```

*(Repeat up to five. Fewer, well described, beats more listed thinly.)*

Even Ground then writes a closing card tying them together — how one child or
family moves between programmes. Nothing needed from the partner for that, though
**a sentence on how the programmes connect** is welcome if the answer isn't obvious.

---

## §6 · Photographs

**7 to 9 images total.** This is the part that most affects how the page feels, and
the part most often under-supplied.

| Slot | Count | Orientation | Minimum size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hero** — one strong image, people foremost | 1 | Landscape, 3:2 | **1500px wide** |
| **Gallery** — the work as it actually looks | 6–8 | Mostly landscape; 1–2 portrait is good | **1400px on the long edge** |

What works, from the pages already built:

- **People, mid-activity.** Learning, farming, teaching, playing. Not posed rows, not empty buildings, not logos.
- **Variety across the set** — one wide establishing shot, some close, at least one adult-and-child together, at least one of the physical place.
- **Originals, not exports.** Straight from the camera or phone. No text, watermark, date stamp, or filter — the site adds its own treatment.

For each image, supply:

```
  Filename:
  What is happening / who is in it:     (becomes the alt text — one line)
  Photographer credit:                  (if one is required)
  Consent on file for identifiable people, children especially?   yes / no
```

**Any image of an identifiable child needs consent on file.** If that is unclear
for a photograph, leave it out and send another — a page can be excellent with
six images and cannot be published with one we're unsure of.

---

## §7 · Even Ground's role — *Even Ground writes this*

Nothing needed from the partner. Included here only so the partner can see what
will appear on their page and object if anything is wrong. It states how long the
support has run, whether it is unrestricted, whether it is multi-year, and what
non-financial support goes with it.

**Please do confirm:** the year the partnership began, and whether the funding is
described accurately.

---

## §8 · A story from your work — *optional but strongly wanted*

Four of the five pages carry one. It is consistently the part visitors remember.

One person, named, whose life the work has touched:

| Field | Length |
|---|---|
| **Name** | as they wish to be credited |
| **Their story** | **70–80 words** — where they started, what changed, where they are now |
| **How the programmes made that possible** | **50–60 words** — the pathway, plainly told |
| **A photograph of them** | portrait or environmental, ≥1200px |
| **Consent** | confirmed in writing, including use of their name and photograph |

Write it as you would tell a colleague. Specifics beat adjectives: what she grows,
how far he walks, what the family eats now.

## Notes for whoever assembles the page

- Word counts above are measured from the live pages: tagline 9–20, lead 20–40,
  background paragraphs 25–60 each, programme descriptions 12–34, Even Ground's
  role paragraph 34–58, story 69–76, pathway ~59.
- The four numbers are `data-target` values on `.counter` spans — integers only,
  with `data-suffix` for `+` or `%`.
- Images: hero `≥1500×1000`, gallery `1400px` long edge, converted to WebP on the
  way in. Keep the original in `.image-originals/`.
- Partner-supplied words are used **verbatim**. Trim if too long; never rewrite.
  If a field comes back unusable, ask the partner again rather than filling the
  gap in-house — see `CLAUDE.md` on copy provenance.
- A page publishes only once its partner has signed off: procedure in
  `HANDOVER.md` §7.
