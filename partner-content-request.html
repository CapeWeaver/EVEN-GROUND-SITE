What we need from you

Your page on evenground.org is already drafted from your organisation's own published material. This form is how you confirm it, correct it, and fill the gaps — so the words on the page are yours, not our paraphrase.

8 sections 450–600 words in total 7–9 photographs Most of it already exists in your annual report
Download the form (PDF, print-ready) Download as plain text

The PDF is seven pages, laid out for A4, and prints cleanly on any printer, so it can be filled in by hand and photographed or posted back. The plain-text version is for typing into an email. Pass either on to anyone who can't work online.

How this works

Every partner page uses the same eight sections in the same order, so the five pages read as one family. You'll receive this form with the current draft text already filled into each field, plus a link to your draft page.

  1. Read each field. If the draft is accurate, leave it.
  2. If it's wrong, out of date, or not how you'd put it — replace it.
  3. Send the photographs, which are the part we can't draft for you.

Word counts are the real measurements from the pages already built. They're targets rather than limits, but something three times the length won't fit the design and will come back to you for cutting.

§2

Four numbers

Four headline figures with short labels. They count up as the visitor scrolls, which sets two rules.

  • One number each, and a whole one. A plain figure, optionally with + or %. Not a range, not “approx.”, not a sentence.
  • Say what period it covers. “Meals served yearly”, “books borrowed in 2024” — a number without a timeframe can't be checked, and we won't publish one.
  • Three is fine if a fourth would be padding. Never more than four.
1.  Number: ______   Label: ______________________   Period: ________
2.  Number: ______   Label: ______________________   Period: ________
3.  Number: ______   Label: ______________________   Period: ________
4.  Number: ______   Label: ______________________   Period: ________
One of the four is usually the relationship itself — “16 · Years of Even Ground partnership”. We can fill that one in.

§6

Photographs

Seven to nine images. This is what most affects how the page feels, and the part most often under-supplied.

SlotHow manyOrientationMinimum size
Hero — one strong image, people foremost1Landscape, 3:21500px wide
Gallery — the work as it actually looks6–8Mostly landscape; one or two portrait is good1400px long edge
  • People, mid-activity. Learning, farming, teaching, playing. Not posed rows, not empty buildings, not your logo.
  • Variety across the set. One wide establishing shot, some close, at least one adult and child together, at least one of the physical place.
  • Originals, not exports. Straight off the camera or phone. No text, watermark, date stamp or filter — the site applies its own treatment.
For each image

Any photograph of an identifiable child needs consent on file. If that's unclear for an image, leave it out and send another. A page is excellent with six photographs and cannot be published with one we're unsure of.
